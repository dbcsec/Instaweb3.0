Welcome

A small inn in Cascade Locks — the Gorge at your doorstep.

Columbia Gorge Inn is an independently run motel in Cascade Locks, Oregon, right in the middle of the Columbia River Gorge. Rooms are entered from the outside, so you park at your door and step straight in. Each room has its own vanity sink and mirror, and several open onto a private patio with seating.

We keep things straightforward: a comfortable bed, a quiet night, and easy access to everything the Gorge is known for. No lobby maze, no long walk with your bags.

Why people come here.

We're in Cascade Locks, in the middle of the Gorge — the waterfalls, the historic highway, the river and the trails are all part of the trip.
