Welcome

Simple, clean rooms — and the Gorge at your doorstep.

Columbia Gorge Inn is a small, independently run motel in the Columbia River Gorge. Rooms are entered from the outside, so you park at your door and step straight in. Each room has its own vanity sink and mirror, and several open onto a private patio with seating.

We keep things straightforward: a comfortable bed, a quiet night, and easy access to everything the Gorge is known for. No lobby maze, no long walk with your bags.

The Columbia River Gorge

Why people come here.

The Gorge is a national scenic area along the Columbia River — waterfalls on the Oregon side, wind on the water, and trails in every direction.

Multnomah Falls

The Gorge's best-known waterfall, and the historic highway that strings the smaller falls together along the way.

Hood River & the wind

Hood River is the West Coast's windsurfing and kiteboarding town — steady summer wind on the river and a walkable downtown.

Hiking & trails

Waterfall trails, ridge climbs and riverside paths — from short family walks to full-day routes above the river.

Trail conditions, seasons and drive times vary — ask when you call and we'll point you the right way.
