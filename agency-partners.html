FAQs

How are leads verified?
Every lead goes through a triple-verification process: (1) phone format and area code validation, (2) email domain MX record check, (3) website URL resolution check. Leads that fail any check are flagged separately.
What format do the leads come in?
CSV and JSON. Compatible with every major CRM including Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive. You can also request custom formats.
Can I choose specific states or cities?
Yes. You can filter by state, city, industry, or any combination. Our batch system lets you build custom datasets. Contact us for custom requests.
How often is the data refreshed?
Weekly. Subscribers automatically receive fresh data every 7 days. One-off buyers get a single snapshot with the option to purchase updates.
